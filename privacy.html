Privacy Policy

How AI Athletics collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

AI Athletics ("AI Athletics", "we", "us", or "our") provides AI-powered sprint analysis tools for athletes, coaches, and sports teams. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you use the AI Athletics application, website, and related services.

By using AI Athletics, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use AI Athletics, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Account credentials and authentication information
  • Profile information you choose to provide

Video and Performance Data

When you use our sprint analysis features, you may upload videos of yourself or athletes. These videos may be processed to generate movement analysis, performance measurements, movement insights, and related feedback.

Depending on the features you use, we may also process performance-related information such as sprint measurements, movement metrics, analysis results, annotations, and progress over time.

Purchase and Subscription Information

If you purchase a subscription or credits through the app, purchases are processed through the relevant app store, such as Apple App Store or Google Play.

AI Athletics does not receive or store your full payment card details. We may receive information necessary to identify your purchase, subscription status, credits, and transaction status.

Technical Information

We may collect technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as device information, application version, operating system information, and technical logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only for purposes related to providing and improving AI Athletics, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Providing sprint video analysis
  • Generating movement and performance insights
  • Storing and displaying analysis results
  • Tracking performance and progress over time
  • Processing subscriptions and credit purchases
  • Providing customer support
  • Maintaining security and preventing abuse
  • Improving the reliability and functionality of the service

4. AI and Video Processing

AI Athletics uses automated processing and AI-based technologies to analyse uploaded sprint videos and generate performance and movement-related insights.

The results generated by AI Athletics are intended as performance and training insights. They should not be considered medical advice, medical diagnosis, or a substitute for assessment by a qualified healthcare professional.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or provide access to information to service providers that help us operate AI Athletics. These providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to maintain appropriate security and confidentiality.

Examples may include providers used for authentication, database hosting, cloud infrastructure, video or AI processing, payment and subscription management, and technical services.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect our rights or users, or in connection with a legitimate legal or security requirement.

6. Payments and Subscriptions

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed through the relevant platform provider, including Apple App Store or Google Play.

AI Athletics may use RevenueCat to manage subscription and in-app purchase information across supported platforms. RevenueCat may process information such as application user identifiers, purchase information, subscription status, and product information in order to provide these services.

7. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain user accounts, fulfil legitimate business purposes, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

Specific retention periods may depend on the type of information and how the service is used.

9. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your AI Athletics account and associated personal information directly through the AI Athletics application or by contacting us.

The following sections explain how to delete your account and what happens to your associated data.

9.1 Who this applies to

This process applies to anyone with an AI Athletics account, whether you use the app as an athlete (player) or as a coach.

9.2 Delete your account in the app

You can request deletion yourself at any time from within the AI Athletics app:

  1. Open the AI Athletics app and sign in.
  2. Go to your profile (your own account, not another athlete's profile).
  3. Open profile settings (edit profile).
  4. Scroll to Delete account.
  5. If you have an active subscription billed through Google Play or the Apple App Store, cancel that subscription first in the store's subscription settings. The app may block deletion while a subscription is still active.
  6. Tap Delete account, then type your account email address exactly as shown to confirm.
  7. Tap Schedule deletion.

After you confirm, your account is hidden immediately from other users. For example, coaches or athletes you were linked with will no longer see your profile or shared content in the app.

9.3 Grace period and permanent deletion

  • Your account and associated app data are scheduled for permanent deletion 30 days after you confirm deletion.
  • During those 30 days you may sign in again and choose Cancel deletion if you change your mind.
  • After the 30-day period, deletion is final and cannot be undone from the app.

9.4 What we delete

When your account is permanently removed, we delete or anonymise app-held data linked to you, including, as applicable:

  • Your profile (name, email on profile, club and profile details)
  • Your authentication account
  • Sprint videos and related files stored for your account
  • Measurements, analysis results, KPI data, and annotations tied to your account
  • Coach–athlete links involving your account
  • Other in-app content you created that is stored under your user ID

9.5 What we may keep and why

We do not sell your personal information. In some cases we may retain limited information after deletion:

  • Legal and security requirements: information we must keep to comply with law, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, or protect security and abuse prevention, only for as long as necessary.
  • Anonymised or de-identified records: for example, internal transaction or credit-ledger entries that no longer identify you by name or email but are kept for accounting or fraud prevention.
  • App store and payment platforms: subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play, the Apple App Store, and our subscription partner RevenueCat. We do not store your full payment card details. Purchase and subscription history may remain with those providers according to their policies. Cancel subscriptions in the relevant store if you no longer want billing.
  • Backups: encrypted backups may retain data for a limited time until they are overwritten in the ordinary course of operations.

9.6 If you cannot use the app

If you cannot access the app but still want to delete your account, contact us and include the email address registered to your AI Athletics account. We may need to verify your identity before processing the request.

Email: privacy@ai-athletics.nl
Or use our Contact page (subject: "Account deletion request").

We aim to respond to verified deletion requests within one month, as required under applicable data protection law, including the GDPR where it applies.

9.7 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability where applicable. Account deletion in the app is one way to exercise your right to erasure.

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the Netherlands, this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens ( autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl ).

Data controller: AI Athletics, Netherlands. For privacy questions, see our Privacy Policy.

9.8 Apple App Store and Google Play

AI Athletics provides an in-app account deletion process for users who have created an account. This page provides additional public information about how to delete an account and the associated data.

Publishing this information on our website does not replace the ability to request account deletion from within the app.

10. Children's Privacy

AI Athletics is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without the required consent, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

11. International Data Transfers

Depending on the services and infrastructure used to operate AI Athletics, personal information may be processed or stored in countries outside your country of residence.

Where required by applicable law, we take appropriate measures to ensure that international transfers of personal information are subject to appropriate safeguards.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, or legal requirements.

When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how AI Athletics processes personal information, please contact us through our Contact page.
